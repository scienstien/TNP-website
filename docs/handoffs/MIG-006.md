## Scope statement
Company identity, cycle-specific directory projection, stale contact saves and Unknown PVR defaults in Lane B company files only. Depends on MIG-002.

## Summary of behavior change (before → after, user-visible and data-level)
Name-based saves and inferred batch cycles become UUID saves and an explicit existing-cycle selector. Company-row locks prevent stale overwrites. The projection consistently uses the offer's cycle and UG department; no cycle shows global contacts only. Unknown PVR remains NULL.
