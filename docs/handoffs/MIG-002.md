## Scope statement
Lane B implements MIG-002, MIG-006, MIG-007 and MIG-008 sequentially in company, drive and calendar modules and their routes/components only. This first task removes company-side event writes, rejects visit-date payloads, retires unused calendar visit actions and makes the directory projection read-only. Lane A files, shared schema/auth/navigation, TASKS.md, AGENTS.md and db/README.md remain frozen; documentation requests go here for the integrator.

## Summary of behavior change (before → after, user-visible and data-level)
Contact saves previously moved/cleared every company PPT and could invent a cycle/drive. They now change only company contacts; visit-date keys are rejected on create/update, and the editor renders the projection as read-only.
